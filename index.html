About （个人简介）
BIO

Hello, I’m Menghui (Murphy Ong). I like observing the world while on the road. Travel, music, guitar, hiking, sports, and the subtle emotional connections between people and cities continually spark new feelings and thoughts.

Rather than standard answers, I prefer exploring the unknown. Whether it’s content, products, AI, visual expression, or authentic human interactions, I’m always curious about “why it is the way it is”, and I actively learn new knowledge and methods.

I’m currently moving from a design & content background toward product thinking. Past experiences made me realize that, beyond creating visuals, I care more about:
How people express, connect, and find a sense of reality in the digital world.

This website is my long-term journal—about travel, content, products, creation, and my ongoing observation of the world.

Hello，我是梦晖，我喜欢在路上观察世界。旅行、音乐、吉他、徒步、运动、人与城市之间细微的情绪连接， 都会让我持续产生新的感受与思考。

这个网站更像是我的长期记录。关于旅行、内容、产品、创作，以及我对这个世界持续进行的观察。
